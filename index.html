Diagnosis

Where the bookings are leaking

Four things are quietly costing you booked safaris right now. None of them need a bigger budget to fix. They need the budget pointed differently, and made measurable.

Leak 01Reach, not intent

Your ad money is aimed at scrolls, not searchers

Your live search ads run roughly 41 display placements to a single search ad. The travelers typing “luxury Kenya safari cost” or “Serengeti migration package”, the ones ready to book, barely see you. You are buying attention from people browsing, not buyers deciding.

Display / awarenessSearch / intent

What it costsBudget spent on impressions and interest, not on booking ready demand.

Leak 02Blind on ROI

You cannot see cost per booking

Tracking is spread across several overlapping analytics setups, and ad links pass through shorteners that break the trail. Clicks are visible. A booking cannot be traced back to the channel that caused it. You are steering a high ticket business on the one number that matters least.

What it costsNo channel is held accountable, so budget cannot move toward what actually books.

Leak 03First seconds lost

A slow first impression on a $10,000 decision

Your pages carry heavy load for a purchase this considered. The trust critical first seconds, the moment a paid visitor decides whether you look like the operator for a once in a lifetime trip, are spent waiting. Paid clicks arrive, then leave before the story lands.

What it costsYou pay for the click, then lose the visitor before the pitch begins.

Leak 04Fuel sitting idle

Your strongest assets are not working for you

A 5.0 reputation and a strong presence on a safari marketplace where travelers actively compare operators are not being turned into retargeting, on page proof, or lookalike audiences built from real guests. This is your best fuel, and it is parked.

The upsideThe trust you have already earned can feed acquisition the moment it is put to work.
